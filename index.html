What we do

8 ways in

Each one has its own mood, its own people and its own colour.

Cine Samvaad

संवाद samvaad = conversation

Films are the excuse. Conversation is the point.

Painting & art classes

रंग rang = colour

Workshops and classes with Mohini Sinha. No experience needed.

Rishtey

रिश्ते rishtey = relationships

No stage. No judges. No applause. Just a circle.

Patna Debate Circle

बहस bahas = debate

Healthy debate, unlike what you see on TV news.

The Reading Club

किताब kitaab = book

Monthly. Open. No exam at the end.

Jamming: Sing Along

गाना gaana = song

No auditions. No pressure. No judgement.

Open mic nights

घर ghar = home

A stage that feels like home, with friends Tape a Tale and Awaken Words.

Write-It-Out

लिखना likhna = to write

No grades. No red pens. No wrong answers. Just write.

House rules

Nobody gets graded here

No experience needed

Never painted, debated or told a story out loud? Wonderful. Everybody starts somewhere, and most people start nervous.

All ages, all kinds

Our painting workshops have welcomed teenagers and a 70-years-young participant at the same table. Students, teachers, doctors and entrepreneurs share the debate floor.

Phones down, people up

In an age of endless scrolling, we're old-fashioned on purpose: real conversations, in a real room, with real chai-level warmth.

The people

An artist and a poet opened a room in Patna

Mohini paints and teaches. Gaurav writes and teaches communication. In June 2026 they opened Kreativ Beings so the rest of us would have somewhere to do the same.
