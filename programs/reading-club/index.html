The Reading Club

Kitaab means book. At The Reading Club, the book is only the starting point.

Unlike a traditional book club that dissects a single title, The Reading Club is about open conversation inspired by whatever you are reading: books, ideas, personal stories and different ways of seeing the world. There are no right or wrong answers.

Once a month on a Sunday afternoon, phones go down and readers of every kind gather: avid readers, people trying to get back into reading, and the simply curious. A different member moderates each chapter, so every month feels a little different.

In November 2026 the club was featured in the Times of India, Patna edition, in a story on the city's growing offline reading culture.

Read. Reflect. Share.The Reading Club motto

Good to know

Do we all read the same book?

No. Bring whatever you are reading and share what it made you think or feel.

I haven't read much lately. Can I still come?

Absolutely. People trying to get back into reading are some of our favourite members.
