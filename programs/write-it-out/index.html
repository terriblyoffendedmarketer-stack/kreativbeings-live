Write-It-Out

Kyunki likhna zaroori hai: because writing matters. Write-It-Out ran online for over a year before it came to the studio.

Write-It-Out is a guided creative writing workshop led by Gaurav Sinha. Through engaging prompts and simple exercises, you write, explore ideas and find your own voice, in Hindi, English or both.

Beginner, poet, storyteller, blogger, or simply someone who wants to express themselves better: there is no pressure to write perfectly.

Bring a notebook, an open mind and your stories.

The best writers don't imagine more. They observe more and write more.From the Write-It-Out invite
