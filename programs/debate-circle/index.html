Patna Debate Circle

बहस किसी को हराने के लिए नहीं, बल्कि ख़ुद के अज्ञान को मिटाने के लिए की जाती है. We debate not to defeat someone, but to clear our own ignorance.

Patna Debate Circle brings healthy debate back in fashion. Students, teachers, doctors, entrepreneurs and the plain curious meet once a month to argue about things that matter, with a moderator, a timekeeper and good manners.

Recent topics: are social media influencers replacing real journalists? Should healthcare, education and railways be privatised? Are Bihar's colleges fulfilling young people's dreams, or driving them away? The very first circle chose this one unanimously: is spending your prime years on government exams dedication or a trap?

The best moments are when people argue against their own beliefs and walk out with a new perspective. First-timers are very welcome.

जहाँ शोर ख़त्म होता है, वहाँ तर्क शुरू होता है।Where the noise ends, reasoning begins.

Good to know

I've never debated before. Can I join?

Yes. Beginners and experienced debaters are equally welcome.

What do you debate?

Education, society, technology, politics and current affairs. Topics are announced with each chapter.

Which language is used?

Whatever you're comfortable in: Hindi, English or a mix.
