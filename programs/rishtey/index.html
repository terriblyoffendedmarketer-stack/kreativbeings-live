Rishtey

A storytelling circle, by invitation of the heart.

rishtey रिश्ते
= relationships, the bonds between people
Rishtey

Har rishta ek kahaani hai. Every relationship is a story, and every story holds a truth nobody said out loud.

Not every story needs a stage. Some only need a safe circle. Rishtey is a gathering for the threads we carry: the thank-you never said, the apology that never found its moment, the bond that healed us or hurt us.

No performances. No judgments. Sirf insaan aur unki kahaaniyan: just people and their stories. A gentle facilitator holds the thread, and you can speak or simply listen.

The first circle was so full we had to add seats. Someone looked around the room and said, "Patna badal raha hai." Patna is changing. We like to think so too.

The threads we carry, spoken aloud.From the Rishtey poster

Good to know

Do I have to speak?

No. You can come only to listen. Both are equally welcome.

Is this an open mic?

No. There are no performances, no judges and no applause.

Why are seats limited?

A circle only works when everyone can hear everyone.
