Jamming: Sing Along

Gaana aaye ya na aaye, gaana chahiye: whether or not you can sing, you must sing. That's the only rule at Jamming.

Bathroom singer, professional musician or someone who just loves listening: this evening is for you. Picture guitars, a cajón, familiar melodies from Kishore Kumar to Coke Studio, and a room full of strangers singing together.

At the first Jamming, grown-ups queued for the mic with child-like energy, and several genuinely good singers surprised themselves. It's music, masti and good vibes, the good old-fashioned way.

Bring your favourite song. Somebody will know the words.

Some evenings are not about perfect singing, but about singing your heart out.From the November invite
