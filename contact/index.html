Good to know

Do I need experience?

No. Every program welcomes first-timers.

Can children join?

Yes. The Kids Art Class is for ages 5 to 12, and painting workshops are open from age 10.

Which languages do you use?

Hindi and English, and plenty of Hinglish.

How do I book?

Most events are ticketed on BookMyShow and District by Zomato. You can also DM us on Instagram.
