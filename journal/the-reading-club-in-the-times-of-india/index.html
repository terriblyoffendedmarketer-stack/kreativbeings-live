The Reading Club in the Times of India

In an age of endless scrolling, it is heartening to see people rediscovering the joy of reading, meeting and connecting offline.

The Reading Club at Kreativ Beings found a mention in the Times of India, Patna edition, as part of a feature on the growing culture of offline reading communities in the city. The article said what we deeply believe: readers want meaningful offline connection, and reading clubs are becoming spaces for conversations that go beyond books.

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who has been part of The Reading Club journey. Every book shared and every thought exchanged made this possible. See you at the next chapter.
