About

A studio, not a classroom

Kreativ Beings, The Art Space, opened its doors on 27 June 2026 on AN Path in Patliputra Colony, Patna. It is a room for self-expression, open dialogue and the slow, satisfying business of making things together.

Think of it as a place away from the daily rush. A room where a nervous first-timer, a school student, a working professional and a 70-years-young painter can all sit down, try something, and say what they really think without being shouted down.

Since opening, the studio has hosted painting workshops, film screenings, debates, reading circles, storytelling circles, sing-alongs and open mics. People leave with a canvas, a sharper argument, a new book to hunt down, or occasionally a new friend. We count all of these as wins.

The owners

Mohini Sinha

Owner, artist and art educator

Mohini spent a decade as a software engineer at companies like TCS, HCL and Cognizant before the forgotten love for art "came back, searching for me." She started painting seriously in 2019 and went full-time in 2022.

Today she is an acrylic artist, a certified Mithila artist and a Skillshare teacher, and she leads every painting workshop at the studio. Her oldest creative memory is painting Goddess Lakshmi on the wall at Diwali, encouraged by her grandfather.

  • Exhibited at ICCR Kolkata, Chitrakala Parishath Bengaluru, AIFACS Delhi and Art Carnival Budapest
  • Certified Mithila artist and Skillshare teacher
  • Instagram: @mohiniartgallery

Gaurav Sinha

Owner, author, poet and communication teacher

Gaurav writes Hindi poetry and short stories and teaches communication, which is a grown-up way of saying he cares about how people say things and whether anybody is really listening. That care runs through every conversation the studio is built around.

His book Shabd Kam Arth Zyada Ho, a collection of 100 Hindi poems, wears one of Mohini's paintings on its cover. Since 2025 he has run Write-It-Out, a bilingual creative writing workshop that began online and now also meets at the studio, and he hosts The Creative Zindagi Podcast.

His motto, from his own bio: ज़िंदगी जीयें कुछ इस तरह, मुस्कुरायें बेतकल्लुफ़ बेवजह! Live life so that you smile freely, for no reason at all.

  • Author of Shabd Kam Arth Zyada Ho, 100 Hindi poems
  • Poems in the anthology Kavita 250
  • Host of The Creative Zindagi Podcast
  • Instagram: @gauravsinhawrites
